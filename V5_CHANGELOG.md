# CarWise V5 AI Mock Data Expansion — Changelog

## Purpose
A large fixture pack for frontend visual development of AI features, multimodal evidence, animation demos, and polished mobile states.

## New content
- Vision fixtures: exterior, interior, engine, wheels, paint, glass, lights, undercarriage
- Copilot fixtures: questions, answers, followups, seller prompts, inspection prompts, price prompts, safety prompts, summaries
- Evidence fixtures: photos, video clips, voice notes, documents, timelines, annotations, quality states, capture guides
- Market fixtures: price bands, comparables, trends, geographic markets, mileage curves, deal signals, valuation explanations, listing cards
- Repair fixtures: repair items, labor, parts, severity impact, maintenance forecasts, scenarios, cost waterfalls, explanations
- Inspection fixtures: checklist, test drive, fluids, tires, brakes, electrical, safety, service history
- Trust fixtures: confidence, uncertainty, human review, audit events, verification, provenance, AI labels, disclaimers
- Motion fixtures: scanning, analysis, scores, charts, sheets, capture motion, success, error motion
- Demo fixtures: journeys, vehicles, personas, edge cases, offline, permissions, subscriptions, showcase cards
- Deterministic mock AI scenario engine
- V5 AI showcase screen
- Previous V4 baseline included in this archive

## Static validation
The final archive was syntax-checked with Node.js `--check` across every JavaScript file contained in the package.
